# PM Cards PWA

This is a mobile-first Progressive Web App prototype.

## Important
A PWA must be served over HTTPS. Do not open index.html from the iPhone Files app.

## Easiest deployment
Upload the contents of this folder to a static HTTPS host such as GitHub Pages, Netlify, or Vercel.

Then open the HTTPS URL in Safari and choose:
Share -> Add to Home Screen

After the first load, the app has a service worker and can work offline.

## Prototype controls
- Tap card to reveal
- Swipe left after revealing to move forward
- Swipe right to go back
- Again / Got it
- Shuffle
- Progress is saved locally on the device
